SimRacing Expo 2026 ran from 16 to 18 October at Messe Frankfurt, and most big hardware brands used it to show what is coming next: some products are on sale now, others are 2027 prototypes.

This round-up sticks to what the manufacturers themselves have confirmed. Every price and spec below comes from the brand's own website or press release, checked on 19 October 2026. If a brand has not announced something, we say so.

## What happened at SimRacing Expo Frankfurt (16–18 October 2026)

The headline themes were clear:

- **More direct drive for less money.** Asetek put a firm price and date on its DD10 wheelbase.
- **Direct drive comes to more consoles.** Moza showed its first wheelbases licensed for PlayStation 5.
- **Wheels with big screens.** Fanatec gave an early look at a steering wheel with a 5-inch colour display.

## Asetek DD10: €499 direct drive, launching 27 October

Asetek confirmed that the DD10 wheelbase launches on **27 October 2026 at 11:00 CET**. The price is **$499 excluding VAT, or €499 including 19% VAT**, as stated in Asetek's expo recap (15 October 2026).

### 10 Nm, upgradeable to 14 Nm with the Boost Kit

According to Asetek's DD10 product page (checked 19 October 2026):

- **Torque:** 10 Nm, or 14 Nm with the Asetek Racing Wheelbase Boost Kit, which is sold separately
- **Power supply:** 96 W
- **Encoder:** 14-bit, 16,384 steps per turn
- **Weight:** 7.92 kg
- **Warranty:** 60 months

### Xbox only with an Xbox-compatible Asetek wheel

The DD10 supports PC. For Xbox, Asetek says you need an Xbox-compatible Asetek Racing steering wheel. The base alone does not make your setup Xbox-ready, so check the wheel before you buy. Asetek does not list PlayStation support for the DD10.

## Asetek GT Nova wheel (March 2027, €299.99)

Asetek also revealed **GT Nova**, a new GT-style steering wheel. It launches in **March 2027** at **$299.99 excluding VAT, or €299.99 including 19% VAT** (Asetek, 15 October 2026).

Its features, as Asetek describes them:

- A 1.3-inch white OLED screen (128×64 pixels) for live data such as gear and speed
- "MultiLink", where one rotary switch changes what the other controls adjust, turning two controls into 24 inputs
- Settings for the wheelbase, pedals and wheel that you can change on the wheel itself, without opening the software on your PC

On top of that, Asetek introduced a **5-year warranty** on all Asetek Racing products bought after 15 October 2026.

## PXN S16 and S23: first hands-on in Europe

PXN used Frankfurt for the first European hands-on sessions with its **S16** direct drive wheelbase. In its press release of 13 October 2026, PXN lists the S16 at **16 Nm of constant torque**, with a 24-bit magnetic encoder and a torque sensor.

The bigger **S23**, rated at **23 Nm constant torque**, was on display only. PXN also had its **VD6 Bundle** to try: wheel, base and pedals in one box, with 6 Nm constant and 7 Nm peak torque.

PXN's press release does not give prices or release dates for the S16 or S23.

## Moza brings direct drive to PS5 (R5S Pro, R16S Ultra)

Moza first showed its PlayStation 5 line-up at Tokyo Game Show on 17 September 2026 and brought it to Frankfurt. It consists of three products:

- **R5S Pro Racing Simulator:** wheelbase, steering wheel and pedals in one package
- **R16S Ultra Racing Simulator:** the R16S wheelbase plus a steering wheel
- **R16S Ultra Direct Drive Wheel Base:** the base on its own

Moza says this is its first officially licensed hardware for PS5, with native support for Gran Turismo 7. Two things are still open. Moza has **not yet announced prices, torque figures or a release date**. It also notes that the products will work with PS5 **subject to Sony's final product approval**.

## Fanatec Project Obsidian and Trak Racer

Fanatec and Trak Racer, both part of Corsair, shared one stand. In their 16 October 2026 press release, Fanatec showed:

- **Project Obsidian:** a prototype steering wheel with a 5-inch colour display under one piece of glass, RGB-backlit buttons and an all-aluminium body. It can send telemetry over USB or Wi-Fi. The launch target is 2027, with no price yet.
- **ClubSport Formula V3 Oracle Red Bull Racing:** a licensed wheel with a 2.7-inch OLED display, due in mid-November 2026.
- **ClubSport Pedals V4:** an early look at a redesigned mid-range pedal set, also planned for 2027.
- **Podium Pedals Sport:** a two-pedal set, on sale from the Fanatec website since 16 October.
- **ClubSport F1 Racing System:** its European debut. It is built around the ClubSport DD+ (18 Nm), which is licensed for PlayStation.

Trak Racer showed two TRZ motorcycle simulators, licensed racing simulators and Hot Wheels-branded cockpits.
