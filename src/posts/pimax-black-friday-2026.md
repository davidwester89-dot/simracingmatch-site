Pimax has started its Black Friday sale for 2026. It runs from **16 November to 5 December 2026** and cuts the price of several VR headsets. Below are the euro prices on Pimax's EU store, what is discounted and what is not.

All prices in this article are **excl. VAT**. The EU store shows the price of the product only; VAT and any local taxes are added at checkout. For the Netherlands, Pimax applies 21% VAT.

## Pimax Black Friday 2026 at a glance

- **Sale period:** 16 November to 5 December 2026.
- **What is discounted:** VR headsets only. Bundles and accessories stay at their regular listed price.
- **Shipping:** free shipping during the sale, for the first delivery of your order. Return shipping is not included.
- **Returns:** new Pimax headsets bought on Pimax.com have a 14-day return guarantee. For returns that are not about a quality issue, you pay the return shipping.
- **Codes:** the Black Friday discount cannot be combined with other promotions, discount codes or special offers, unless Pimax explicitly says otherwise.

## Headset prices on the EU store (excl. VAT)

These are the prices on the [Pimax EU store](https://eu.pimax.com/pages/black-friday-2026). For each headset you see the regular price, the Black Friday price and the difference, all excl. VAT.

- **[Crystal Light](https://eu.pimax.com/products/pimax-crystal-light):** €853 → €809 during the sale (€44 less).
- **[Crystal Super](https://eu.pimax.com/products/pimax-crystal-super), QLED 57 PPD or Ultrawide:** €1,459 → €1,319 (€140 less).
- **[Crystal Super 8K Micro-OLED](https://eu.pimax.com/products/pimax-crystal-super-sony-8k-micro-oled):** €1,960 → €1,769 (€191 less).
- **[Dream Air](https://eu.pimax.com/products/pimax-dream-air), SLAM + controllers:** €2,050 → €1,949 (€101 less).
- **[Dream Air](https://eu.pimax.com/products/pimax-dream-air), Lighthouse, headset only:** €1,783 → €1,699 (€84 less).
- **[Dream Air SE](https://eu.pimax.com/products/pimax-dream-air-se):** no discount. SLAM + controllers stays at €1,070, Lighthouse headset only at €802.

The Crystal Super also has a QLED 50 PPD version at the same price, but it was out of stock when we checked.

The Dream Air SE is the newer model and keeps its regular price during the sale. You do get free shipping on it.

## What the Black Friday deal does not cover

Not everything in the Pimax store is part of the sale:

- **Bundles:** a headset bundle stays at its regular listed price. If you want the headset discount, buy the headset on its own.
- **Accessories:** no Black Friday discount, but free shipping still applies.
- **Discount codes:** you cannot stack a code on top of the Black Friday price.

Did you buy a headset on the official Pimax store between 1 and 15 November 2026? Then Pimax's price protection may apply. It covers headset orders placed directly on the store in that period. Check the conditions on the Black Friday page before you contact Pimax.

## Before you order

Three things to check on the store itself:

1. **Your final price.** The prices above are excl. VAT. Your total, with VAT, appears at checkout.
2. **The version.** Crystal Super and Dream Air come in more than one version, at different prices. Make sure the one in your cart is the one you want.
3. **Stock.** Not every version is in stock. Pimax lists shipping times per product on its Shipping Status page.
