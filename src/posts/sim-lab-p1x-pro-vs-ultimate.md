Sim-Lab sells two aluminium profile cockpits under the P1X name: the P1X Pro and the newer P1X Ultimate. They share the same footprint, the same wheel mounts and the same compatibility lists. The difference is in the frame and about €110. Every spec and price below comes from Sim-Lab's own EU store, read on 9 November with Dutch prices in euros, taxes included.

## Quick comparison table

- **Price, cockpit only:** P1X Pro €789.00 · P1X Ultimate €899.00
- **With Integrated Vario™ Single monitor mount:** Pro €979.00 · Ultimate €1,088.00
- **With Integrated Vario™ Triple monitor mount:** Pro €1,089.00 · Ultimate €1,198.00
- **Dimensions (L x W x H):** both "1350 mm x 580 mm (widest point 680mm) x 770 mm"
- **Wheel mounting:** both "Front mount, Wheeldeck, or Fanatec DD mount", at the same price
- **Profiles:** Pro "heavy-duty profiles with an enhanced structure" · Ultimate "fully slotted profiles", "four slots on each side"
- **Uprights:** Ultimate "brings back the iconic straight uprights"
- **Warranty:** both "3-year warranty"
- **Colour:** both black

## Frame and build

The **P1X Pro** is, in Sim-Lab's words, "the evolution of a cockpit known for its excellent rigidity". Sim-Lab combined "heavy-duty profiles with an enhanced structure" and replaced universal corner brackets "with bespoke sandwich plates and pre-drilled holes", which it says makes assembly "much easier and quicker".

The **P1X Ultimate** is "our newest version of the original P1 cockpit". Sim-Lab says it listened to feedback on the Pro: the Ultimate "now features the fully slotted profiles and upright profiles, making it simpler to attach accessories". Its "newly developed, proprietary profiles" have four slots on each side, which Sim-Lab calls "lightweight and highly practical". The straight uprights, according to Sim-Lab, make "mounting your wheel, dashboard, or button box" more intuitive. The pre-drilled holes and sandwich plates of the Pro "are all carried over".

In short: the Pro is built around rigidity, the Ultimate around slots and straight uprights for accessories. Sim-Lab publishes no stiffness or weight figures for either, so we don't compare them on that.

## Mounting: front mount, wheel deck, pedal plate

Both cockpits come with the same three wheel mount choices, and the choice does not change the price:

- **Front mount:** Sim-Lab says the front/side mount "offers the maximum leg room and rigidity for Direct Drive wheels with front mounting holes like Simucube and all Fanatec DD wheels".
- **Wheel deck:** "the most flexible, allowing virtually any wheelbase to be mounted".
- **Fanatec DD mount:** a dedicated mount for Fanatec's direct drive bases.

The box contents are the same for both: aluminium profiles and mounting materials, the wheel deck or front/side mount, brackets for front mount wheels, a pedal deck, a side mount for accessories, bolts for mounting seats and an adjustable feet set. Sim-Lab says the Pro's side mount lets you "easily attach your handbrake/shifter". Seat, seat brackets and seat slider are optional on both. Want to switch mounts later? Sim-Lab sells the P1X Pro Front Mount, P1X Pro Wheeldeck and P1X Pro Podium Bracket Set separately at €99.00 each.

## Price (sim-lab.eu, euro)

On 9 November, Sim-Lab's store listed these regular prices for the Netherlands, taxes included:

- **P1X Pro:** €789.00 without monitor mount, €979.00 with the Integrated Vario™ Single, €1,089.00 with the Integrated Vario™ Triple.
- **P1X Ultimate:** €899.00 without monitor mount, €1,088.00 with the Integrated Vario™ Single, €1,198.00 with the Integrated Vario™ Triple.

So the Ultimate costs €110 more as a bare cockpit and €109 more with either monitor mount. Both can also be ordered with a seat. Those seat bundles showed a lower price without an end date, so we leave them out. Check the delivery time on Sim-Lab's product page before you order.

## Which bases and pedals fit

Sim-Lab publishes one compatibility list per mount, and it is the same for the P1X Pro and the P1X Ultimate. For brands we compare on SimracingMatch:

- **Front mount:** Simucube 2 and Simucube 3 (Sport, Pro, Ultimate) and Sim-Lab's own DDS26 and DDS39 TorqueSync™.
- **Wheel deck:** Asetek Forte, La Prima and Invicta; Fanatec CSL DD, GT DD Pro, ClubSport DD, ClubSport DD+, DD1, DD2 and Podium DD; Logitech G29, G920/G923, G Pro and RS50; Moza R3, R5, R9 V3, R12 V2, R21 Ultra and R25 Ultra; Thrustmaster T248, T300/TX, T500RS, T-818 and T598; and Simucube 3.
- **Fanatec DD mount:** DD1, DD2, CSL DD, DD Pro, ClubSport DD, ClubSport DD+ and Podium DD.
- **Pedals:** among others Sim-Lab XP1, Asetek Invicta, Forte and La Prima, Fanatec ClubSport V3 and CSL Pedals LC, Logitech G PRO and RS pedals, Moza CRP, SR-P and mBooster, Simucube ActivePedal and Thrustmaster T-LCM. Sim-Lab notes the Moza CRP2 is "compatible with loose pedals only".

## Which one fits you

Using only the facts above:

- **Choose the P1X Pro** if you want the lower price for the same footprint, mounts and compatibility. It is €110 cheaper as a bare cockpit.
- **Choose the P1X Ultimate** if you plan to add a dashboard, button box or other accessories. Its fully slotted profiles and straight uprights are what Sim-Lab changed to make that easier.
- **Either way,** pick the mount by wheel base: front mount for Simucube or a Fanatec DD with front holes, the Fanatec DD mount for Fanatec, the wheel deck for nearly everything else.
